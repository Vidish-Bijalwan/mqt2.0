## The 5 strategic findings

1. **Head terms are unwinnable; combinations are the business.** The winnable
   surface is destination × origin × duration × audience × month × budget.
2. **Origin-city pages are the proven playbook** (competitors rank
   `/tour-packages/from-kolkata-to-andaman/`, `vaishno-devi-tour-package-from-delhi`).
   Prioritize tier-2 origins (Lucknow, Indore, Surat, Nagpur, Chandigarh) where
   competition is weakest.
3. **Freshness beats authority** for registration/permit/date queries: Char Dham
   registration, Kedarnath heli (Phata/Sersi/Guptkashi), Amarnath (Baltal vs
   Pahalgam), safari zones/prices, festival dates (Hornbill, Pushkar, Rann Utsav),
   live status ("is kedarnath open now").
4. **Assistance positioning for official bookings** (Char Dham, HeliYatra/IRCTC,
   SASB, TTD, safaris, Bhasma Aarti): never imply MQT is the official portal —
   "assistance + package around it" language only.
5. **Underserved audiences:** senior citizens (~9% of MMT package bookings),
   corporate/MICE, pet-friendly, women-only/solo group departures.

## Cheapest keyword goldmine (build first)

Instrument `search_query` (raw query, no PII), `whatsapp_click` with context, and
UTM capture on enquiries — real user language beats any third-party list.
See `../analytics/LEAD_FUNNEL.md`.

## Cluster → URL discipline

Every strategic cluster maps to **one preferred URL** (`SEARCH_INTENT_MAP.md`).
New pages must not cannibalize existing ones; origin × destination pilots stay
**noindex until quality is proven** (real route differentiation required — city-name
swaps are doorway-class).
