Best Time to Visit Badrinath Kedarnath Dham

The best time for the Kedarnath and Badrinath Do Dham Yatra is in summer and off-monsoon periods. In these seasons, you're greeted with cool and pleasant weather. The skies are clear, for the most part, which makes the temple tours and sightseeing a lot easier.

Right after the monsoon ends, the mountains turn lush green - perfect for photography and peaceful Darshan. Another practical advantage: accommodation rates drop in September-October as crowds thin out.

  • Summers: May to June
  • Off-monsoon season: September to October

We strongly advise against taking this trip during the monsoon (July to August) and winter seasons (November to April). During monsoons, you'll often find road blockages and landslides. In winter, the shrines are closed because of heavy snowfall.

Legends of Kedarnath and Badrinath Temples

Adi Shankaracharya, the 8th-century saint and philosopher, is credited with the establishment of Badrinath Dham and the restoration of Kedarnath Temple. Here are the legends and myths associated with the Badrinath Kedarnath Dham:

Badrinath Temple

As per Hindu mythology, Lord Vishnu was once meditating in Badrinath under a Badri tree. There was immense cold during that time but the God was deep into meditation and paid no heed to the harsh weather. When Goddess Laxmi knew about this situation, she took the form of a Badri tree to protect him from the icy weather.

When Lord Vishnu came to know about this, he became very pleased with her devotion and venerated her with the name Badri and he himself came to be known as Badrinath!

In today's day and age, Badrinath is considered the abode of Lord Vishnu and many people book their Badrinath Kedarnath Yatra Package to visit the sacred temple as well as admire the stunning Himalayan landscapes all around!

Kedarnath Temple

As per the legends, Pandavas once sought Lord Shiva for forgiveness for the sins they committed during the Kurukshetra War. However, God was in no mood to grant them forgiveness so easily. So in order to avoid them, he took the form of a bull and fled.

Bhima, the most powerful of the Pandavas, chased the bull. To avoid being caught, Lord Shiva, in the bull form, took a dive into the ground and disappeared! However, his hump was left behind at the very location of Kedarnath. And this made this site an immensely sacred place for Shaivites.

Many pilgrims do a collective Badrinath Kedarnath Yatra from Delhi to seek the blessing of both the prime Hindu deities.

Badrinath Kedarnath Yatra Registration 2026

  • Registration is mandatory for visiting Kedarnath and Badrinath in 2026.
  • Register free at: registrationandtouristcare.uk.gov.in
  • You will need your Aadhaar card and mobile number for OTP.
  • Carry the printed or digital e-pass at all checkpoints.
  • Without registration, entry to the temple can be denied.

Kedarnath Trek Guide

  • Trek Route: Gaurikund → Kedarnath (16 km one way)
  • Trek Time: 6-8 hours. Difficulty: Moderate to Challenging
  • Altitude: Gaurikund (1,982m) → Kedarnath (3,583m)
  • Alternatives: Pony, Palki, or Helicopter from Phata/Sirsi
  • Start early - by 4:00-5:00 AM to avoid afternoon cloud cover and rush

Altitude Sickness Warning

  • Gain of 3,300+ metres in altitude - significant risk for first-timers
  • Symptoms: headache, nausea, dizziness, breathlessness
  • Prevention: drink 3-4 litres water/day, walk slowly, rest well on arrival
  • Medical camps available at Sonprayag, Gaurikund, and Kedarnath
  • Senior citizens (60+) must carry a doctor's fitness certificate

What to Pack

  • Documents: Aadhaar, Yatra registration e-pass (printed), travel insurance
  • Clothing: thermals, fleece jacket, waterproof windcheater, woollen cap, gloves
  • Footwear: trekking shoes with grip (essential for Kedarnath trek)
  • Medicines: personal meds, basic first-aid, ORS, altitude sickness tablets if prescribed
  • Essentials: power bank, torch, cash (ATMs scarce near Kedarnath)

Top Things to Do on Your Kedarnath Badrinath Do Dham Yatra

This pilgrimage is one of the holiest tours you can undertake in your life. And it's only fitting that you make the most out of your trip by doing the following wholesome activities on your Delhi to Badrinath Kedarnath tour Package:

1. Seek Blessings at Badrinath and Kedarnath Temple

Kedarnath Temple is one of the 12 Jyotirlingas and the Badrinath Shrine is part of both the Chhota Chardham and Bada Chardham circuit. It's also believed that one who seeks Darshan here is very close to attaining Moksha!

2. Attend Evening Aarti and Other Rituals at Badrinath and Kedarnath

Attend the divine Rudra Abhishek at Kedarnath in the morning. Also, witness the grandeur of Badrinath Maha Aarti & Kedarnath evening Aarti after the sun sets and experience the spiritual atmosphere created by the repeated chants of God's name - the highlight of your Badrinath Kedarnath Yatra Package!

3. Take a Holy Dip at Tapt Kund in Badrinath

The Tapt Kund residing near Badrinath Temple is believed to have therapeutic and healing properties. Bathing in this hot water spring is a pretty standard ritual before proceeding for Badrinath Darshan.

4. Visit Mana Village

Explore the Mana Village countryside, filled with serene and picturesque sites at every turn of the corner. The two star locations of the village are Vyas Gufa and Bheem Pul.

5. Shop for Souvenirs at Badrinath and Kedarnath

Shop for the kind of spiritual things you'd find only here! There are several markets in both Badrinath and Kedarnath where you can buy souvenirs like Rudraksha beads, Shivlings, Ganga Jal, idols, Malas, etc.

Kedarnath Badrinath Do Dham Yatra is one of the holiest of all pilgrimages in India. If someone is unable to plan a complete Chota Char Dham Yatra, then they must at least explore the shrines of Do Dham.

Best Places to Visit on Badrinath Kedarnath Trip

The two-fold Yatra to Badri Kedar Dham has a plethora of sites for you to discover and explore. Holding immense spiritual significance, these sites are a must-add:

Badrinath Dham and Kedarnath Dham

These sacred shrines are the star destinations of your Badrinath Kedarnath Do Dham Package. Do the Badri Kedar Darshan and offer your heartiest prayers to the temples' highly revered deities.

Guptkashi

You'll stumble upon this sacred town on your way to Kedarnath. It's the place where Lord Shiva actually set foot on when he came here to hide from Pandavas. You'll also find the famous Ardhanarishwar Temple and Vishwanath Temple here.

Sonprayag

Don't miss the site where the holy waters of the Rivers Mandakini and Basuki meet! Pilgrims often come here to take the coveted holy dips.

Joshimath

When you can't visit the Badrinath Dham in the snowfall season, come to Joshimath, as this is the winter seat of Lord Badrinath. It offers you a lot of spiritual as well as outdoor fun activities, such as skiing and other snow sports.

Rudraprayag

Venerated as one of the Panch Prayags, this place witnesses the holy confluence of the Alaknanda and Mandakini Rivers. Get your camera ready as the whole scene and the majestic Himalayan peaks make for a postcard-worthy picture.

Gaurikund

The sacred place marks the starting point for the 16 km trek to Kedarnath. The hot spring waters of the Gaurikund are incredibly sacred to Shakti devotees as Goddess Parvati is believed to have meditated here for thousands of years to win the love of Lord Shiva back.

Devprayag

This is yet another one of the Panch Prayags and deserves a mention in your Badrinath Kedarnath Yatra Package itinerary. The place marks the meeting point of the holy rivers of Alaknanda and Bhagirathi. This is especially auspicious since the sacred confluence gives rise to the incredibly holy Ganges.

Tour ItinerarySee More

Day 1: Delhi – Haridwar

Your Do Dham Yatra will commence from the time you arrive in New Delhi. Our tour representative will transfer you into your transportation that will drop you off at your first destination in Haridwar which is also known as the 'gateway to Char Dham' since all the Char Dham Yatras begin from Rishikesh and Haridwar. This will be a road journey of around 5-6 hours as you have decided to cover Badrinath Kedarnath Yatra by Car.

On arrival in Haridwar, you shall be checked into your hotel or accommodation of your choice. If you want then you can also attend the iconic Ganga Aarti at Har Ki Pauri. You shall be staying overnight at the hotel here.

Places You’ll See

Day 2: Haridwar – Guptkashi

After a delicious breakfast in the morning, you will be driven to your next destination of Guptkashi which is around 230 kms away from Haridwar. It will be an extremely scenic drive which will be one of the most scenic experiences of your life. You will reach there by lunch or pre-dinner time and will immediately be checked in to your hotel here for an overnight stay.

If there is time for you to explore or if you are not tired much then you can even visit the famous Kashi Vishwanath Mandir here. The best option would be for you to go on a soulful leisurely walk and explore the surroundings nicely. You will be staying at your allotted hotel in Guptkashi.

Places You’ll See

Day 3: Guptkashi – Sonprayag – Kedarnath

Early in the morning as the sun rises you shall be driven to your next destination for the day to Sonprayag. This is will be a short drive where you will be served lunch in between and will take just half an hour to 45 minutes to cover depending on the traffic.

Sonprayag is a key checkpoint and registration point on the Kedarnath route. From Sonprayag, pilgrims take a shared jeep or taxi to Gaurikund (approx. 5 km) - which is the actual base camp and starting point of the 16-18 km Kedarnath trek.

From here you will be able to choose to go on foot or hire some alternative method to travel like ponies or Pithu service. On reaching Kedarnath you will be staying in one of the tents or the hall accommodations here. After settling in, head to the temple for evening Darshan. Kedarnath Temple closes at approximately 8:00-9:00 PM after the evening Aarti. The post-Aarti Darshan is deeply peaceful - plan your visit accordingly.

Places You’ll See

Day 4: Kedarnath – Sonprayag – Rudraprayag

Early in the morning, you will be taken for the morning Aarti Darshan of Baba Kedar. The spirituality of this place when paired up with the purity of the morning sun rays is one experience that you need to have as words will not do justice. After this holy Darshan of Baba Kedar, you will be your trek back to the starting point of Sonprayag will begin.

Here you will meet a driver who will then transfer you to your next destination of Rudraprayag which is around 60 kms away. It is one of the holiest Panch Prayags of Uttarakhand - the sacred confluence of the Mandakini and Alaknanda rivers. From here the Alaknanda flows further downstream, eventually meeting the Bhagirathi at Devprayag to form the holy Ganga.

On reaching Rudraprayag you will be transferred to your allotted choice of accommodation here for an overnight stay.

Places You’ll See

Day 5: Rudraprayag – Badrinath Dham

Early in the morning after breakfast, you will be transferred to your next destination of Badrinath via a car ride. Traveling through the hills guarantees picturesque vistas during your road trip. On your way, you will be made to visit and offer Darshan at Shri Narsingh Temple which is situated in the holy town of Joshimath that lies just before Badri Vishal.

After covering a distance of 158 kms and finally arriving in Badrinath you will be checked into your hotel here for an overnight stay. In the evening after freshening up or if you are in the mood to explore your surroundings you will be taken for a brief sightseeing tour of tourist attractions in Badrinath. This will include Vyas Gufa, Mana Village (3km away), Saraswati River, Bheem Pul, etc.

After completing your Badrinath Yatra if you want then you can attend the evening Aarti procession at Badri Vishal Temple. Later on, you will be made to head back to your accommodation in Badrinath town for an overnight stay.

Places You’ll See

Day 6: Badrinath – Rudraprayag

You will be made to visit the holy Badrinath temple early in the morning to attend the holy Aarti ceremony here. After the completion of your Darshan and puja rituals, you will be driven to your next destination for the day which will be Rudraprayag.

After covering a scenic journey, you will be made to check in to your hotel for an overnight stay. The remainder of the day will be free for you to indulge in some leisurely activities like strolling around the holy village and relaxing amidst its scenic beauty. You shall be staying for the night in Rudraprayag.

Places You’ll See

Day 7: Rudraprayag – Rishikesh

Your journey to Rishikesh will begin early in the morning after your breakfast. You will be driven to Rishikesh via a car journey which you will remember for a long time. You will cover a journey of around 133 kms and on arriving at Rishikesh you will be checked into your allotted hotel for an overnight stay.

In the evening you will have a fun time in attending the holy Ganga Aarti at any of the ghats of Rishikesh.

Places You’ll See

Day 8: Rishikesh – Delhi Departure

Your journey back home will commence early in the morning from Rishikesh via the road. It will be a trip of around 5-6 hours and you will be dropped off at your departure location at the New Delhi railway station. This final drop-off will mark the end of your Do Dham pilgrimage to Kedarnath and Badrinath.
