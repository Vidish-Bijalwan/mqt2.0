# Digital PR Strategy — My Quick Trippers

Digital PR is about earning high-authority backlinks and brand mentions by creating newsworthy content or providing expert commentary to journalists.

## 1. Reactive PR (HARO / Connectively)
**Goal:** Answer journalist requests for travel experts.
- **Platform:** Sign up for Connectively (formerly HARO - Help A Reporter Out) or Qwoted as a "Travel Expert".
- **Action:** Monitor queries related to "India travel", "post-pandemic tourism", "pilgrimage travel trends".
- **Execution:** When a query matches, have Rajesh (or your designated expert persona) reply quickly with a unique insight.
- **Result:** A quote in a major publication (e.g. Forbes, Times of India) with a DR 80+ backlink to MQT.

## 2. Proactive Data PR (The "Hero" Campaign)
**Goal:** Create a proprietary data study that journalists *want* to cite.
- **Concept:** "The State of Chardham Yatra 2026" or "Top 10 Emerging Honeymoon Destinations in India".
- **Execution:** 
  1. Analyze internal booking data or survey past customers.
  2. Create a visually appealing report page on `/blog/chardham-trends-2026` with charts (infographics).
  3. Pitch the story to travel journalists at Indian news outlets (Moneycontrol, The Hindu, local state papers).
- **Why it works:** Journalists need data for their stories. If you provide the data, they link to your report as the source.

## 3. Newsjacking
**Goal:** Capitalize on breaking news.
- **Scenario:** The government announces a new highway to a pilgrimage site, or a new Vande Bharat train route to Kashmir.
- **Action:** Quickly publish a blog post on how this impacts travel. Pitch your "expert take" on the economic impact to regional journalists via Twitter/X or email.

## 4. Influencer Partnerships
Instead of just paying for posts, run a PR campaign with influencers.
- **Campaign:** "The 12 Jyotirlinga Challenge"
- **Action:** Sponsor a mid-tier YouTuber or Instagrammer to cover the 12 Jyotirlinga tour in 15 days.
- **Result:** User-generated content, YouTube backlinks (highly trusted by Google), and cross-promotion.

## Execution Timeline
- **Month 1:** Set up HARO/Qwoted monitoring. Pitch 3 times a week.
- **Month 2-3:** Compile and publish one proprietary data study. Build media list of 50 travel journalists. Pitch the study.
- **Ongoing:** Maintain relationship with journalists who cover Indian tourism.
