# Topical Authority Map — My Quick Trippers

## 1. What is Topical Authority?
Google ranks sites higher when they demonstrate exhaustive expertise on a specific subject, rather than just having one target page. We achieve this using the **Hub and Spoke (Pillar Cluster) Model**.

## 2. Core Pillars (The Hubs)
These are your Destination Pages (e.g. `/destinations/kashmir`). They should be the ultimate guides for the destination.

## 3. The Clusters (The Spokes)
These are long-tail Blog Posts (`/blog/[slug]`) that link back to the Pillar page.

### Example Cluster: Kashmir
**Pillar Page:** `/destinations/kashmir`

**Supporting Spoke Content (Blogs to write/optimize):**
1. *When is the best time to visit Kashmir for snow?* (Informational)
2. *Gulmarg vs Pahalgam: Which is better for families?* (Comparison)
3. *What to pack for a Kashmir winter trip?* (Checklist)
4. *Top 5 romantic houseboat stays in Srinagar* (Listicle)
5. *Is it safe to travel to Kashmir right now?* (Trust/Safety)

**Internal Linking Rule:** Every single one of these spoke posts must contain a contextual link back to `/destinations/kashmir` and relevant `/packages/kashmir-...` pages (AutoLinker does this automatically if configured right!).

### Example Cluster: Pilgrimage (Chardham)
**Pillar Page:** `/india-tours/chardham`

**Supporting Spoke Content:**
1. *Chardham Yatra Route Map Explained*
2. *Helicopter vs Road: Best way to do Chardham Yatra*
3. *Medical fitness requirements for Amarnath & Kedarnath*
4. *How to register for Chardham Yatra online*

## 4. Execution Plan
1. **Audit current 410 blogs:** Categorize them into clusters (Kashmir, Kerala, Chardham, Bali, etc.).
2. **Identify gaps:** If Bali has a destination page but 0 blogs, write 5 supporting blogs for Bali.
3. **Siloing:** Ensure blog posts primarily link up to their parent pillar, and across to sibling posts in the same cluster. Do not excessively cross-link between unrelated clusters (e.g. don't link a Bali post to a Chardham post unless naturally relevant).
