# Backlinks & Off-Page SEO Strategy — My Quick Trippers

## 1. The Strategy: Quality over Quantity
As an established brand with a new domain/architecture, we need high-DR (Domain Rating) links that signal trust to Google. Do not buy bulk spam links (Fiverr etc.) — these will trigger a manual action.

## 2. Link Building Avenues

### A. Tourism Boards & Government (DR 70+)
- **Target:** State tourism directories (e.g. Uttarakhand Tourism, MP Tourism, Goa Tourism)
- **Action:** Since MQT India is a registered/approved agency (ISO 9001:2008), apply to be listed in the "Approved Tour Operators" directories of state tourism websites. These `.gov.in` and `.nic.in` links are the holy grail of travel SEO.

### B. Travel Bloggers & Influencers (DR 30-60)
- **Target:** Indian travel bloggers writing about specific niches (e.g. "Chardham Yatra Guide", "Honeymoon in Bali").
- **Action:** Pitch a value exchange. Offer them a discounted trip or affiliate arrangement in exchange for an in-content backlink reviewing your specific package.
- **Link placement:** Inside the text, e.g., "For a hassle-free trip, I recommend the *[12 Jyotirlinga Tour by MQT India](link)*."

### C. Local SEO Citations (DR 40-90)
- **Target:** JustDial, Sulekha, YellowPages India, TripAdvisor, TrustPilot.
- **Action:** Ensure Name, Address, Phone (NAP) consistency across all profiles. These aren't always "dofollow", but they establish entity trust.
- **Google Business Profile:** Ensure the New Delhi, Hyderabad, and Pune offices all have verified profiles linking back to `myquicktrippers.com`.

### D. Resource Pages (DR 40-70)
- **Target:** Universities, expat forums, or corporate HR portals that list "Trusted travel partners for India tours".
- **Action:** Outreach with your ISO certification and company history.

## 3. The "Unlinked Brand Mention" Campaign
MQT has been around for 10+ years and served 50k+ customers.
- **Action:** Set up Google Alerts for "My Quick Trippers" and "MQT India".
- **Execution:** Find blogs/news sites that mentioned the brand without linking to the website. Email the webmaster politely asking them to turn the mention into a link.

## 4. Toxic Link Audit
- **Frequency:** Quarterly via Ahrefs or Semrush.
- **Action:** Disavow any highly toxic, spammy, or adult links pointing to the domain.
